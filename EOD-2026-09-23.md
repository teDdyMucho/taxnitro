EOD: TaxNitro – the assistant answers and raises tickets, and two accounts turned out to have no profile

1. The chat assistant works end to end now. It answers from the written procedures, and it did not before — it only greeted, because the client's question was never reaching it.

3. Tickets reach Slack. Tried the whole path — troubleshoot, narrow down, last try, then a ticket — and it stopped raising a second one for the same conversation.
4. Three things were wrong with the tickets and are fixed: the reply arrived wrapped in quotation marks, the ticket was raised on the first message instead of after three, and "channel:" was being written inside the message where the channel name belongs in its own field.
5. It escalated far too eagerly at first. That was my instruction, not the model's fault — I had told it to raise a ticket immediately for anything technical.
6. It also counted three suggestions in one message as three attempts. It gives one thing to try now and waits for the answer.
7. The ticket used to carry whatever the client last typed, so a ticket read "still cant". It summarises the whole conversation now and quotes what they actually said.
8. Every ticket carries its own reference, TKT-260923-K3P9 style, in the Slack message and in what the client is told.
9. Added the chat to the staff portal. It was only ever in the client one — different navigator, and I missed it.
10. Staff get a different assistant: it reads the internal procedures, calls itself Support, and raises bug reports rather than support requests.
11. Wrote a procedure for a client who cannot upload, from what actually blocks the upload button. The first reason on the list is the commonest: no document type picked yet, which leaves the file picker locked.
12. Button names arrived as **Upload** with the asterisks showing. The chat draws them bold now rather than asking the assistant to stop formatting.
13. Added a reset button to the chat. It clears the conversation and starts a new session, so the assistant stops believing it has already raised a ticket.
14. Angelo said Edit Profile did nothing. It was saving — there was simply no profile row to save into.
15. Two accounts exist in Auth with no profile: Angelo's from June, and a test1 account from August. Their names show as "User", they have no client id, and any edit they make goes nowhere.

17. Edit Profile says "Not saved" now when a save reaches nothing, instead of closing as though it worked. That silence is what made this take as long as it did.
