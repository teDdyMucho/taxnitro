Finance Therapy Group — Portal Update

For clients

1. Each bank account you're set up with now appears as its own item on your Required Documents checklist, with its own upload slot and approval.
2. The upload box now lists your own required items instead of a fixed set of Tax folders.
3. Files sent through the sidebar Upload button now update your checklist — they previously did not.
4. Drag and drop files, send several at once, and see them sorted by type before sending.
5. Download one file, a whole folder, or tick the ones you want; several files arrive as one .zip.
6. After 5 minutes of inactivity you're signed out, with an on-screen note explaining why.

For staff and admin

7. Add a client's bank accounts (bank name and last 4 digits) when creating or editing them.
8. A client's documents now appear in folders matching what they chose when uploading.
9. Drag and drop, multiple files at once, grouped by destination folder, with confirmation before removing.
10. Single, whole-folder, and select-and-download available on every document screen.
11. Workflows can now be edited or deleted, with a confirmation listing what gets removed.
12. The All Documents screen no longer overlaps on phones.

No action needed — existing clients are unaffected until bank accounts are added to their profile.
